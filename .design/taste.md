# Taste

**It is:** calm, exact, fast, trustworthy. A signal light, not a slot machine.

**It is not:** noisy, gamified, a chat inbox, a dashboard of widgets, vague "AI magic".

## Lines not to cross
- No number on screen that the parser did not read from the request.
- The AI recommends; it never decides. The founder's gesture is the only trigger.
- Every action has a 3-second undo and an audit row.
- Never show a green badge when the evaluation fell back; show `FLAGGED`.
- Demo and sandbox data are labelled as such wherever a judge might mistake them for real.
- No secrets, real names or real emails in screenshots or video.

## Voice
Plain, short, active. Say what happened and what to do next. No exclamation marks.

## Motion
Almost none. The 3-second countdown is the one moving thing on the glasses. Dashboard transitions 150–200 ms, ease-out, no bounce.

## References to study
LifeLine's `.design/` set for how a brief, a taste file and a design system fit together; the Even Hub catalogue for what existing G2 apps look like, so Greenlight is clearly different.
